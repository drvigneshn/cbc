    Post-cardiac-arrest care

    🫁 Airway ✓
    Confirm airway & ETT position · confirm ETCO₂ · secure tube.
    🫀 Breathing ✓
    Titrate O₂ (SpO₂ 94–99%) · avoid hyper/hypoventilation · target normal ETCO₂.
    ❤️ Circulation ✓
    BP & perfusion · treat hypotension · consider fluid bolus / vasoactive infusion.
    🧠 Neuro ✓
    Neuro exam · check glucose · targeted temperature management · treat seizures.
    🔍 Cause ✓
    Identify & treat the cause — Hs & Ts · 12-lead ECG · labs · imaging.
