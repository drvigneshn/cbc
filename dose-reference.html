Code Blue Companion — Dose Calculation Reference

All formulas used by the app · aligned with AHA PALS 2020/2025 & APLS · created by Dr Vignesh N, Kanchi Kamakoti CHILDS Trust Hospital · matches app v4.0

For verification & teaching. Every dose must be independently confirmed against your unit's protocol before administration. Worked examples use a 10 kg child. Doses are per actual body weight; the app caps each at the maximum shown.

Milrinone0.25–0.75 µg/kg/minload 50 µg/kg over 30–60 minthen per unit protocolload 500 µg

Rule of 6: 3 × weight (mg) in 50 mL gives 60 × weight µg/mL, so 1 mL/h = 1 µg/kg/min. The 0.3 × weight version is 10× more dilute (1 mL/h = 0.1 µg/kg/min) for the potent agents. Prefer your unit's standardised concentration where one exists.
