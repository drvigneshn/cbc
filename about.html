What it does

🩺
Rapid assessment
Guides responsive · breathing · pulse, and tells you if it’s an arrest.
💉
Weight-calculated doses
Every arrest drug, RSI agent, infusion and equipment size, auto-calculated and capped.
⚡
Arrest console & timers
Cues adrenaline, reassessment and shocks as they fall due — with optional voice read-out.
📋
Code log
Records every action with a timestamp; saved securely for later review.

Guidelines & references

Aligned with AHA PALS 2020/2025 and APLS weight estimates. A trained clinician must verify every dose against local protocol.

AHA PALS — cpr.heart.org ↗ IAP India — BLS / PALS ↗ Dose calculation reference →
Decision-support aid, not a substitute for clinical judgment. This tool assists recall and calculation; the treating clinician must verify every dose before administration and remains responsible for all decisions.
